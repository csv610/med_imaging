\chapter{Common Imaging Artifacts Across Modalities}
\label{app:imaging_artifacts}

Imaging artifacts are features appearing in an image that do not correspond to true anatomy or pathology. Recognizing artifacts prevents misdiagnosis and guides corrective acquisition steps.
