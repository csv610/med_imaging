\chapter{Standardized Reporting Systems (RADS) Quick Reference}
\label{app:reporting_rads}

Standardized Reporting and Data Systems (RADS) provide uniform terminology, risk stratification, and management thresholds. Each lexicon applies only to its validated organ, modality, and clinical population.

\section{Critical-Result Communication Principles}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Closed-loop communication:} Urgent or unexpected life-threatening findings (e.g., tension pneumothorax, intracranial hemorrhage, aortic dissection, bowel perforation, malpositioned endotracheal tube) require direct verbal communication with the treating team.
    \item \textbf{Documentation:} Record the name of the clinician notified, the date, and the time in the final radiology report.
\end{itemize}
